## Fetchly 0.1.0

The first release of Fetchly: download YouTube videos to your own computer, in the quality you choose. Everything runs on your machine. There is no account, no server in between, and nothing is installed system-wide.

### Get started

1. Download **Fetchly.zip** below and unzip it.
2. Double-click **start.bat** (Windows) or **start.command** (Mac).
3. Your browser opens Fetchly. Keep the small window open while you use it; close it (or press **Quit**) to stop.

The first start takes a few minutes: Fetchly downloads itself (about 8 MB) and then its helper tools (about 300 MB). After that it starts in seconds.

**Windows:** if you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
**Mac:** right-click **start.command**, choose **Open**, then click **Open** again. You only need to do this once.

### What you can do

- Paste a YouTube link, see the title, length and thumbnail, and pick a quality: anything from 144p up to 4K or 8K when the video has it, or **Audio only**.
- Queue several downloads and watch their progress, speed and time left.
- Cancel a download at any time. Unfinished pieces are cleaned up.
- Download the same video in more than one quality. Each gets its own file, named with the quality, for example `My video [abc123] 1080p.mp4`.
- Open the download folder, or show a finished file in Explorer or Finder, straight from the app.
- Choose your download folder and default quality in **Settings**, and see your download history.

### How it behaves

- **Files that play everywhere.** Videos are saved as MP4, preferring H.264 so they open in any player. Above 1080p, YouTube only offers VP9 or AV1. Audio only is saved as M4A, exactly as YouTube delivers it.
- **A tidy download folder.** While a download runs, its pieces stay in a hidden `.cache` folder inside your download folder. Only the finished file appears.
- **Problems explained in plain words.** A failed download is retried twice. If it still fails, Fetchly says why (private video, not available in your country, no internet, and so on) and pauses the queue so you notice.
- **Updates only when you say so.** Fetchly checks for newer versions of its tools and offers them in **Settings**. Nothing is installed without your click, and each tool can be rolled back to its previous version.
- **Private by design.** Fetchly runs only on your computer and only accepts connections from it. It connects to YouTube for your downloads, and to GitHub and the tool download sites to install and check for updates.

### Requirements

- **Windows 10 or 11**, 64-bit.
- **macOS** on Apple Silicon or Intel (preview, see below).
- About 400 MB of free space for the app and its tools, plus room for your videos.

### Known limitations

- **macOS is a preview.** The Mac version is built and packaged, but it has not yet been tested on a real Mac. Please report anything that goes wrong.
- **Single videos only.** Playlists, channels and live streams aren't supported yet.
- **No signed-in content.** Private, members-only and age-restricted videos can't be downloaded.
- **Unsigned app.** Windows and macOS show a one-time warning the first time you open it (see Get started).

### Third-party software

Fetchly downloads these tools from their official sources and checks each download against its published SHA-256 checksum before using it:

- [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp): Unlicense
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org): GPL builds, from BtbN on Windows and Martin Riedl on macOS
- [Deno](https://deno.com): MIT

Only download videos you have the right to download.
