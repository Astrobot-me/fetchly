Fetchly - download videos to your computer
==========================================

STARTING THE APP

  Windows:  double-click  start.bat
  Mac:      double-click  start.command

A small window opens and your web browser shows the app. Keep the small
window open while you use Fetchly. To quit, close that window or press
Quit in the app.

The first start takes a few minutes: Fetchly downloads itself (about
8 MB) and then its helper tools (about 300 MB). After that it starts
in a few seconds.


FIRST TIME ON WINDOWS

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then
"Run anyway". This only happens once.


FIRST TIME ON A MAC

macOS may say start.command "cannot be opened" or is from an
"unidentified developer". To open it:

  1. Right-click (or Control-click) start.command and choose Open.
  2. Click Open in the message that appears.

This only happens once. If the Open button is missing, go to
System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
"Open Anyway" next to the message about start.command.


WHERE THINGS ARE

  Your videos:   the Downloads folder, unless you choose another one
                 in Settings.
  App files:     Windows  %LOCALAPPDATA%\ytgui
                 Mac      ~/Library/Application Support/ytgui

To remove Fetchly, quit it and delete the app files folder and the
folder that holds these start files. Your videos are not touched.


UPDATES

Fetchly never updates itself without asking. Open Settings and press
Update when you want the newest versions of its tools.

Only download videos you have the right to download.
